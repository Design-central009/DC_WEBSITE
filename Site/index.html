FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you handle design and construction together?+

Yes. Architecture, structural design, interiors and building contracts sit under one brand, so one team takes your project from idea to handover.

Which areas do you serve?+

Our studio is in Golden Heights Colony, Attapur, Hyderabad. We take on projects in all areas of Hyderabad and outside the city too, with recent projects in Bangalore and Goa.

How does a project usually begin?+

With a conversation about your site, needs and budget, followed by a site visit and an initial design direction before detailed planning.

Do you take on interior-only work?+

Yes. We design interior spaces for comfort and visual appeal, whether or not construction is part of the scope.

How long does the design stage take?+

It depends on scale and complexity. A typical home design stage runs a few weeks; we confirm a schedule after the first briefing.

How do you control project costs?+

We plan efficient use of space and materials early, share clear estimates and review costs with you at each stage.
